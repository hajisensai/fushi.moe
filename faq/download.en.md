The [download page](/download) picks a working source between the Cloudflare mirror and GitHub automatically, and "Download" pulls over several routes at once; you can also go straight to [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hajisensai/Fushi/releases).

For problems downloading the recommended pack, see [What is the recommended pack?](/faq/pack.en).
