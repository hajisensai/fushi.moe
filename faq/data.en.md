---
title: "Where is my data stored? Is anything uploaded?"
description: "Everything stays on your device. There is no account system and nothing is uploaded; sync is optional."
category: "Troubleshooting"
order: 300
date: 2026-09-19
lang: en
---

Everything lives on your own device. Fushi has no account system, needs no registration, and never uploads your books, dictionaries, progress or card history to any server.

Sync is optional and goes through services you configure yourself — Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox, WebDAV / FTP / SFTP, or Fushi interconnect on your LAN; credentials stay on the device. Details in the [privacy policy](/privacy).
