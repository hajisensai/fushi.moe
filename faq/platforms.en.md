Windows, macOS, Android and iOS — the same experience on all four. The iOS build ships through TestFlight and, per App Store rules, has no search or download features.

There is no prebuilt Linux package yet; you can [build from source](https://github.com/hajisensai/Fushi#building). Installers for every platform are on the [download page](/download).
