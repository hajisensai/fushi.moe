Windows, macOS, Android and iOS — the same experience on all four, with a few differences:

- **macOS**: Apple Silicon (M-series) only; Intel Macs are no longer supported.
- **iOS**: distributed through TestFlight, with updates arriving a few days after the other platforms. Per App Store rules it has no **Browse** module — no Discover page, no online sources (manga / novel / video extensions) and no downloads, so AI download is hidden as well. Local import, media servers, Anki, interconnect and everything else work as usual.
- **Galgame capture** (hooking game text, mining cards with game voice) is Windows-only; other platforms can play games running on your PC through the experimental [game streaming](/faq/galgame.en#playing-pc-games-on-your-phone).

There is no prebuilt Linux package yet; you can [build from source](https://github.com/hajisensai/Fushi#building). Installers for every platform are on the [download page](/download).
