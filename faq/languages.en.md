No. What you can look up is decided by the dictionaries you import — Fushi reads Yomitan, MDX (MDict) and DSL dictionaries, and its deinflection covers many languages.

The recommended pack contains Japanese dictionaries plus Japanese / English pronunciation libraries. Learning something else? Skip the pack and import your own dictionaries in dictionary management. The interface itself comes in 17 languages.
