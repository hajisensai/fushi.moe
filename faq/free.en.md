Yes. Fushi is open source under the [GPLv3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) licence. Every feature is free — no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid unlocks. The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/hajisensai/Fushi), so you can audit it and build it yourself.

The only thing that may cost money is the optional AI features: Fushi doesn't provide an AI service, it uses the provider you configure under Settings → AI, and that provider bills you (a model running on your own machine works too). Not using AI doesn't affect anything else — see [What is AI download?](/faq/ai-download.en).

If it helps you, consider supporting development, distribution and maintenance through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/hajisensai).
